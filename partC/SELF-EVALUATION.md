## 3) What did I learn from this experience?
- AI is strongest for draft generation and option exploration, not final truth.
- Verification (tests + manual review) is the core professional skill.
- Scope control is critical: saying "no" to over-engineering improved quality.
- Honest attribution and AI usage transparency are part of engineering ethics.
